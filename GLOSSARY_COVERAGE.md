# Glossary Coverage Boundary

Content Library version: 1.0.3  
Owner: Bored Polymath Studios

MammoLingo is a selected, independently authored U.S. mammography-report education library. It is not a complete report dictionary, an official implementation of any reporting system, or a source of individual interpretation.

## Included vocabulary groups

| Group | Included examples |
| --- | --- |
| Assessment categories | BI-RADS 0–6, described in original plain language with public sources |
| Exam type and technique | Screening mammogram, diagnostic mammogram, 3D mammography / tomosynthesis, CC and MLO views, compression |
| Image review process | Comparison with prior images, additional views, Findings and Impression sections |
| Common image findings | Breast density, fibroglandular/fatty tissue, calcifications, mass, cyst, fibroadenoma, fat necrosis, asymmetry, architectural distortion, lymph node, skin or nipple observations |
| Relevant context | Breast implant, biopsy clip, surgical clips, post-surgical changes, bilateral/axillary location, stability |

## Intentionally excluded

- Proprietary BI-RADS descriptor taxonomy, subcategories, tables, definitions, illustrations, and management algorithms.
- Ultrasound, MRI, contrast-enhanced mammography, pathology, treatment, or patient-specific decision support.
- Facility-specific templates, abbreviations, incidental wording, and every possible local reporting convention.
- Risk estimates, interpretations of a particular report, and recommendations for a particular person.

## Content method

Every entry is newly composed for MammoLingo. Public FDA, NCI, American Cancer Society, and American College of Radiology pages are used to check factual context; the app does not reproduce source definitions, tables, images, or taxonomies. The content register records each entry’s author, source URL, review date, and independent-composition status.

Clinical review, legal review, and IP clearance remain open governance follow-up work. This document defines a safer coverage boundary; it is not clinical or legal approval.
