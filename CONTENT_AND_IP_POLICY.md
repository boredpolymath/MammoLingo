# Content and Intellectual-Property Policy

## Purpose and status

MammoLingo is an independent educational glossary. It does not interpret an individual report, diagnose a condition, or recommend care. This policy sets the project's internal content and intellectual-property practices; it is not legal advice or a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal review.

## Content scope

The initial release is limited to terms that may appear in U.S. mammography reports. The project may refer to report terminology so readers can identify terms in their own reports. It does not claim to be an official implementation of, or a comprehensive substitute for, any proprietary reporting system or lexicon.

## Independent authorship and sources

- Every explanation must be newly written for this project in plain language.
- Do not reproduce, closely paraphrase, translate, adapt, or reorganize proprietary definitions, examples, tables, illustrations, screenshots, category taxonomies, or other expressive material.
- Use publicly available, authoritative sources to verify factual accuracy, but write the user-facing explanation independently.
- Each published entry must have a source record with its public URL, retrieval/review date, content owner, author/reviewer, and an independent-composition confirmation.
- Each entry should link users to an appropriate public source or state why a public source link is unavailable.

## Names and trademarks

- Names of report standards or terms may be used descriptively only when needed to help users recognize report language.
- Source names and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners. Their use does not imply affiliation, sponsorship, endorsement, or approval.
- When used, identify BI-RADS® as a registered trademark of the American College of Radiology.
- Do not use the American College of Radiology logo or other third-party logos. Do not describe MammoLingo as “official,” “certified,” or an implementation of a third-party lexicon without written authorization.

## Review and escalation

Before publishing or materially changing an entry, verify its source record and independently composed wording. Escalate to counsel before using quotations beyond a minimal, approved use; reproducing or translating standard materials; incorporating images or taxonomies; expanding to new modalities or regions; or making a public worldwide launch. Obtain written permission or a license before reproducing or adapting proprietary lexicon materials.

## Current position

The public app identifies its content as independently written, attributes named trademarks, and disclaims affiliation or endorsement. This is not a legal clearance. Comprehensive coverage of any proprietary lexicon remains out of scope until rights and legal review are resolved.
